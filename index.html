1. Polyphase Potential Field & Traveling Wave Force

An $N$-phase Electrodynamic Dust Shield creates a non-uniform spatial electric potential distribution $V(x, y, t)$ across interdigitated surface electrodes spaced at pitch $\lambda$:

$$V(x, t) = \sum_{m=1}^{N} V_0 \cos\left(2\pi f t - \frac{2\pi x}{\lambda} + \theta_m\right)$$

The continuous time-averaged lateral dielectrophoretic force $\langle \mathbf{F}_{\text{DEP}} \rangle$ propelling uncharged and polarizable lunar grains is proportional to the gradient of the electric field squared:

$$\langle \mathbf{F}_{\text{DEP}} \rangle = 2\pi \varepsilon_0 \varepsilon_m r^3 \operatorname{Re}[K(\omega)] \nabla |\mathbf{E}_{\text{RMS}}|^2$$

When $\theta_m = \frac{2\pi(m-1)}{N}$, the wave travels unilaterally with linear phase velocity $v_p = \lambda f$. If $\theta_m$ deviates, spatial harmonics create standing nodes with zero lateral velocity ($\langle \mathbf{F}_{\text{DEP}} \rangle = 0$).

Key Boundary: Phase angle drift $|\Delta\theta| > 15^\circ$ degrades net lateral clearance speed by $>82\%$.

2. Grain Complex Polarizability & Charge Dynamics

Lunar regolith consists of jagged pyroxene, plagioclase, and nanophase metallic iron ($\text{npFe}^0$). Its dipole frequency response is governed by the Clausius-Mossotti function $K(\omega)$:

$$K(\omega) = \frac{\varepsilon_p^* - \varepsilon_m^*}{\varepsilon_p^* + 2\varepsilon_m^*}, \quad \text{where } \varepsilon^* = \varepsilon - j\frac{\sigma}{\omega}$$

In addition to DEP, triboelectrically charged particles carrying surface charge $q_i$ experience dominant Coulombic repulsion and levitation:

$$\mathbf{F}_{\text{total}} = q_i \mathbf{E}(x, y, t) + 2\pi \varepsilon_0 \varepsilon_m r^3 \operatorname{Re}[K(\omega)] \nabla |\mathbf{E}|^2 - m_i g_{\text{moon}}\hat{\mathbf{z}}$$

Lunar gravity ($g_{\text{moon}} = 1.62\text{ m/s}^2$) allows micron-sized grains ($1\text{--}50\ \mu\text{m}$) to remain levitated in the 1–3 mm sheath layer, provided the electric traveling field retains cohesive directional phase vectoring.

Vacuum Characteristic: In lunar hard vacuum ($\approx 10^{-10}\text{ Pa}$), plasma discharge Paschen limit increases beyond 4 kV at sub-millimeter electrode pitches.

3. Regolith Loading & Phase Angle Skew ($\Delta\theta$)

As jagged dust covers an electrode track, localized dielectric constant climbs from $\varepsilon_r \approx 1.0$ (vacuum) to $\varepsilon_r \approx 2.8\text{--}3.5$. This induces variable capacitive shunt loading $\Delta C_m$:

$$Z_m(f) = R_{\text{trace}} + \frac{1}{j 2\pi f (C_0 + \Delta C_m)}, \quad \theta_m(f) = -\arctan\left(2\pi f (C_0 + \Delta C_m) R_{\text{trace}}\right)$$

Unequal capacitance across tracks shifts the phase separation between phases $A$, $B$, and $C$. The resulting Standing Wave Ratio (SWR) along the coverglass surface becomes:

$$\text{SWR} = \frac{V_{\max}}{V_{\min}} = \frac{1 + |\Gamma_{\text{phase}}|}{1 - |\Gamma_{\text{phase}}|}, \quad |\Gamma_{\text{phase}}| \approx \sin\left(\frac{|\theta_{\text{actual}} - 120^\circ|}{2}\right)$$

When $\text{SWR} \gg 1.5$, stationary nodes develop along the array, permanently trapping grains which bake and sinter under intense direct solar UV radiation ($1361\text{ W/m}^2$).

Failure Mechanism: Uncompensated SWR creates "hot-spots" causing permanent loss of photovoltaic output ($>45\%$ degradation).

4. State Observer Inversion & Adaptive Retuning

RegoShield continuously samples return current $I_m(t)$ and voltage $V_m(t)$ across each phase rail using high-voltage sensing dividers, computing real-time inter-electrode phase:

$$\hat{\theta}_m = \arg\left(\frac{1}{T}\int_{0}^{T} V_m(t) e^{-j 2\pi f t} dt\right), \quad \mathbf{e}_\theta = \begin{bmatrix} \hat{\theta}_B - \hat{\theta}_A - 120^\circ \\ \hat{\theta}_C - \hat{\theta}_B - 120^\circ \end{bmatrix}$$

An onboard FPGA-synthesized digital pulse-width modulator applies feedforward corrective phase offsets $\delta \theta_m(k+1)$ and adjusts excitation frequency $f$:

$$\begin{bmatrix} \theta_{B,\text{out}} \\ \theta_{C,\text{out}} \end{bmatrix}_{k+1} = \begin{bmatrix} \theta_{B,\text{out}} \\ \theta_{C,\text{out}} \end{bmatrix}_{k} - \mathbf{K}_p \mathbf{e}_\theta - \mathbf{K}_i \int \mathbf{e}_\theta dt$$

By maintaining $\mathbf{e}_\theta \to 0$ in under $0.5\text{ ms}$, RegoShield guarantees pure traveling wave propagation ($\text{SWR} \le 1.05$) regardless of regolith accumulation, thermal shock, or micro-crack impedance anomalies.
